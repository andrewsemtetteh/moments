# Logo shortlist: "A moment rising" (Round 2 · 06 refined)

The agreed variations to choose the final Moments logo from. Designs:
https://claude.ai/artifact/437tvNBxiVnkkv6AG79CZA (board "Round 2 · 06 refined").

**Idea:** a heart rising over the horizon like the sun. A sunrise is one
moment in time, it happens every day (like the app's daily moment, question
and streak), the half-risen heart is love still growing, and the horizon is
the same sky you share even when apart. The lines below are its reflection.
