# Moments ❤️

Moments is a private, couples-only relationship platform designed to help partners stay emotionally connected, build memories, communicate deeply, and consistently find meaningful things to do together.

It is not a social network.

It is a **relationship operating system for two people**.

---

# Vision

Modern relationships struggle with:

* Lack of time together
* Emotional distance
* Repetitive conversations
* “What should we do?” fatigue
* Forgetting meaningful moments
* Poor communication habits

Moments solves this by becoming the **daily relationship companion** for couples.

When couples don’t know what to do, want to talk, want to share, want to plan, or want to reconnect — they open Moments.

---

# Product Philosophy

## 1. Private by Design

Moments is not social media.

There are:

* No followers
* No public profiles
* No feeds
* No likes
* No discovery algorithm

Everything exists between two people.

---

## 2. Built for Shared Experiences

The core focus is not messaging — it is **doing life together**.

Moments prioritizes:

* Shared activities
* Emotional expression
* Communication
* Planning experiences
* Memory building

---

## 3. Emotional Continuity

Every interaction contributes to a living relationship timeline:

* What you said
* What you did
* How you felt
* What you experienced together

---

## 4. Low Effort, High Meaning

The app should provide value instantly:

* “We’re bored → here’s something to do”
* “You miss them → send a Moment”
* “No plans → here’s a date idea”

---
