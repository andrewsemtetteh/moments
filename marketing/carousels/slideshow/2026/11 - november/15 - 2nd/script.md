## Slides

1. **Cover:** "What to ask *after an argument*" · "Wait until you're both calm. Then go in this order."
2. Step 1 · Listen first — What did you need from me in that moment? (Don't defend yourself. Just hear it.)
3. Step 2 · Own it — What did I say that hurt the most? (Then apologize for that part, specifically.)
4. Step 3 · Go deeper — What were you really upset about? (It's rarely about the dishes.)
5. Step 4 · Clear the air — Is there anything you still need to say? (Leave space. A little silence is okay.)
6. Step 5 · Plan ahead — What can we do differently next time? (Pick one small change you'll both actually keep.)
7. Step 6 · Reconnect — Are we okay? (Ask it. Mean it. Then hug it out.)
8. **Save:** "Save this for the next hard talk." · "You won't remember these mid-argument. That's what saving is for."
