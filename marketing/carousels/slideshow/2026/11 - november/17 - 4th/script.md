## Slides

1. **Cover:** "Your *attachment style* might explain your arguments" · "Not a diagnosis. Just a lens that makes fights make sense."
2. **Secure** — The steady one. Fight: stays calm, says how they feel, trusts it'll be okay. Helps: keep it up.
3. **Anxious** — The fix-it-now one. Fight: silence feels scary, pushes to solve it now. Helps: reassurance, "we're okay, I just need a minute."
4. **Avoidant** — The needs-space one. Fight: goes quiet, needs time. Helps: space plus a promise to come back to it.
5. **Fearful-avoidant** — The push-and-pull one. Fight: wants closeness and space at once. Helps: patience, consistency, naming feelings.
6. **Save:** "Save this for after your next fight." · "Then guess each other's style. Comment yours."
