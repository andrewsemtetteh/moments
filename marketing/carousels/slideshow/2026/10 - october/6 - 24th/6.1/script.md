## Slides

1. **Cover:** "Small habits of *couples who last*" · "None of them take more than a minute."
2. Say good morning. — Even on busy days. A two-word text still counts.
3. Ask about the small stuff. — How was the meeting? Did the bus show up on time?
4. Say thank you for normal things. — Dinner. The dishes. Remembering your coffee order.
5. Go to bed at the same time. — Even twice a week. The pillow talk is the point.
6. Fight the problem, not each other. — It's the two of you against the issue. Always.
7. Keep a running joke. — The thing only you two find funny. Protect it.
8. **Save:** "Save this and start tomorrow." · "Pick one habit. Do it for a week. Watch what changes."
