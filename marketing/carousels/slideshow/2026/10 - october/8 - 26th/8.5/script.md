# 8.5 — How to stop arguing about chores

- **Date:** 2026-10-26 (post 5 of 5)
- **Pillar:** Communication
- **Design:** peach theme · `k` cards, variant `wheel` · cover `d8_5` (couple + spinner)

## Slides

1. **Cover:** "How to stop *arguing about chores*"
2. Make the invisible work visible — List everything, even “remembering birthdays.”
3. Swap by preference — Hate dishes? Trade them for laundry.
4. Use one shared list — Tick it off. No reminders needed.
5. Do a ten-minute reset — Every night, together, one playlist.
6. Thank each other for chores — Yes, even the boring ones.
7. Let a wheel pick the worst one — Nobody chooses. The wheel decides.
8. **Save:** "Save this *before the next dish debate.*" · "Spin the wheel. No appeals."
