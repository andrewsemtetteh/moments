## Slides

1. **Cover:** "Long distance dates that *actually feel like a date*" · "For when 'so… how was your day?' isn't cutting it anymore."
2. Cook the same recipe. — Pick one dish, shop separately, cook it together on video.
3. Movie night, in sync. — Press play at the same second. Text your reactions.
4. Take a walk together. — Call while you both walk your neighborhood. Show each other around.
5. Send a surprise delivery. — Their favorite snack, at their door, on a random Tuesday.
6. Play a game online. — Trivia, chess or a co-op game. The loser plans the next date.
7. Write actual letters. — On paper, in the post. It'll arrive like a hug.
8. **Save:** "Save this for your next date apart." · "Then send it to the person on the other end of the call."
