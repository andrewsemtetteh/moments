# 9.5 — How to keep the spark after years together

- **Date:** 2026-10-27 (post 5 of 5)

## Slides

1. **Cover:** "How to keep the spark *after years together*"
2. Try something new together — New is the fastest way back to “us.”
3. Flirt like it’s the beginning — Compliments, notes, a wink across the room.
4. Plan dates on purpose — Put them in the calendar. Then protect them.
5. Keep a little mystery — Not every plan needs announcing.
6. Touch for no reason — A hand on their back as you pass by.
7. Talk about more than logistics — Ban “who’s picking up what” for one night.
8. **Save:** "Save this *and strike one tonight.*" · "Small sparks, big fire."
