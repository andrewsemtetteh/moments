# 10.5 — How to support your partner's dreams

- **Date:** 2026-10-28 (post 5 of 5)
- **Pillar:** Support
- **Design:** night theme · `k` cards, variant `mission` · cover `d10_5` (couple + rocket)

## Slides

1. **Cover:** "How to support *your partner’s dreams*"
2. Ask about it often — Interest is fuel.
3. Learn the details — The names, the deadlines, the hard parts.
4. Protect their time — Take something off their plate so they can work.
5. Be their first fan — Share it, show up, clap loudest.
6. Stay calm about setbacks — “What’s next?” beats “I told you so.”
7. Dream together too — Make room for one shared goal.
8. **Save:** "Save this *for their next big idea.*" · "Every dream needs a co-pilot."
