## Slides

3. They're the first person you tell. — Good news, bad news, a weird dream. Them first.
4. Your inside jokes need no context. — One word and you're both crying laughing.

6. They know your usual. — Your food order, your coffee order, your hungry face.
7. You'd pick them as a friend too. — Even without the romance, you'd want them in your life.
