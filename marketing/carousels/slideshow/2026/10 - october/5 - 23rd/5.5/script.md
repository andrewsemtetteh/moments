# 5.5 — Relationship myths, busted

- **Date:** 2026-10-23 (post 5 of 5)
- **Design:** sunny theme · illustration `newspaper`

## Slides

1. Relationship myths, busted — Things everyone says that aren’t actually true.
2. “Couples who fight are doomed.” — How you repair matters more than how often you argue.
3. “They should just know.” — Nobody reads minds. Say what you need, kindly and clearly.
4. “The spark lasts on its own.” — Sparks need fuel: new things, tried together.
5. “You have to like the same things.” — Different hobbies keep you both interesting.
6. “Love means never needing space.” — Time apart makes time together better.
7. “Happy couples never get bored.” — Boring is often just another word for comfortable.
8. Save this and send it to the overthinker. — Which myth did you believe the longest?
