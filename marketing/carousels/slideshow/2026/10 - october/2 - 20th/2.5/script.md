## Slides

1. **Cover:** "Things to say instead of *"calm down"*" · "Small swaps that end arguments faster."
2. "Calm down." → "I can see this really matters to you."
3. "You always…" → "Lately I've noticed…"
4. "Whatever." → "I need a minute. Then let's talk."
5. "I'm fine." → "I'm not okay yet, but I want to be."
6. "You're overreacting." → "Help me understand how it felt."
7. "It's not a big deal." → "It's a big deal to you, so it matters to me."
8. **Save:** "Save this for the next heated moment." · "Then send it to the one who still says 'whatever.'"
