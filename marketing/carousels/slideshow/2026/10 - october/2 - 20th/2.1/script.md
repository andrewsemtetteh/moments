## Slides

1. **Cover:** "7 things that make *long distance* easier" · "From couples who survived the airport goodbyes."
2. Always have a countdown. — A date on the calendar turns "someday" into 23 days.
3. Overlap your routines. — Same coffee time. Same show. Same bedtime call.
4. Send small things, often. — A photo of your lunch beats a two-hour call once a week.
5. Plan dates, not just calls. — Cook the same recipe. Watch a movie in sync. Play a game together.
6. Talk about the hard days too. — Distance gets heavier when you pretend it's fine.
7. Keep your own life full. — Friends, hobbies, plans. You'll have more to share when you talk.
8. Make an "after" list. — Everything you'll do when you're finally in the same city.
9. **Save:** "Save this for the hard weeks." · "Then send it to the person you're counting days for."
