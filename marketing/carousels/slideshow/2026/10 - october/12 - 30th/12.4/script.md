# 12.4 — How to be a better listener

- **Date:** 2026-10-30 (post 4 of 5)
- **Pillar:** Communication
- **Design:** night theme · `k` cards, variant `wave` · cover `d12_4` (couple + headphones)

## Slides

1. **Cover:** "How to be a *better listener*"
2. Put the phone face down — Your attention is the gift.
3. Don’t plan your reply — Just listen until they’re done.
4. Ask “and then what?” — Curiosity keeps them talking.
5. Say it back — “So you felt…” shows you got it.
6. Notice what’s not said — Tone, pauses, the sigh.
7. Remember it later — Bring it up tomorrow. That’s listening.
8. **Save:** "Save this *for your next deep talk.*" · "Listening is a love language too."
