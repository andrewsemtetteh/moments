# 12.1 — 30 truth or dare questions for couples

- **Date:** 2026-10-30 (post 1 of 5)
- **Format:** long list (30, three per slide, truth/dare alternating)
- **Design:** ink theme · `list` strips (cream = truth, coral = dare) · illustration `truthdare`
- **Inspiration:** Paired, Marriage.com and StyleCraze couples truth-or-dare lists (rewritten, brand-safe).

## Cards

1. Truth: What was your first thought when you saw me?
2. Dare: Describe me using only three emojis.
3. Truth: What’s the most embarrassing thing you did to impress me?
4. Dare: Do your best impression of me waking up.
5. Truth: Have you ever pretended to like something because I did?
6. Dare: Serenade me with the last song you played.
7. Truth: Which habit of mine have you secretly copied?
8. Dare: Let me pick your phone wallpaper for a week.
9. Truth: When did you realize you liked me?
10. Dare: Slow dance with me, no music.
11. Truth: What’s something you’ve never told me?
12. Dare: Give me a thirty-second shoulder massage.
13. Truth: What’s your favorite photo of me, and why?
14. Dare: Talk in an accent for the next three rounds.
15. Truth: What’s the weirdest dream you’ve had about me?
16. Dare: Recreate our first kiss.
17. Truth: What do you miss most when I’m away?
18. Dare: Write me a two-line love poem, right now.
19. Truth: Who was your first ever crush?
20. Dare: Show me your most embarrassing playlist.
21. Truth: What would you change about our first date?
22. Dare: Let me style your hair however I want.
23. Truth: What’s the pettiest thing that annoys you about me?
24. Dare: Call me a new nickname for the rest of the night.
25. Truth: What’s the most romantic thing I’ve done?
26. Dare: Draw my portrait in sixty seconds.
27. Truth: Which day with me would you relive?
28. Dare: Plan our next date in under a minute.
29. Truth: What’s your biggest green flag in me?
30. Dare: Tell me five things you love about me. Go.
