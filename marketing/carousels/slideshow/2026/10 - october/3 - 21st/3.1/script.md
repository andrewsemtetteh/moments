## Slides

1. **Cover:** "How to love someone whose love language is *acts of service*" · "No flowers required. Just fewer chores."
2. Do the thing before they ask. — The task they've been putting off all week? Done. Quietly.
3. Make their coffee exactly right. — Their mug, their milk, their sugar. Every single time.
4. Handle one adult thing. — A booking, a bill, that phone call they keep dodging.
5. Refill the small stuff. — Charge their phone. Fill the water bottle. Top up the gas.
6. Take something off their plate. — Ask what you can take on when they're stressed. Then actually take it.
7. **Save:** "Save this for their next busy week." · "Then quietly start with number one."
