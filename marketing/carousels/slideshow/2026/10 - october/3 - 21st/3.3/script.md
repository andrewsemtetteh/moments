# 3.3 — What to do when your partner is stressed

- **Date:** 2026-10-21 (post 3 of 5)
- **Pillar:** Support / care
- **Format:** 6 tips
- **Design:** sky theme · `teabag` slides: tea bag (crimped top, staple, string to a "steep with care" tag) · illustration `backhug`
- **Goal metric:** saves + shares

## Slides

1. **Cover:** "What to do when your partner is *stressed*" · "Small things that help more than you think."
2. Ask: advice or a hug? — Sometimes they want solutions. Sometimes they just want you.
3. Take one thing off their list. — The errand, the email, the dishes. Just one.
4. Make their favorite drink. — No questions asked. Just set it down next to them.
5. Be quiet, but stay. — You don't have to fill the silence. Just be close.
6. Plan something to look forward to. — A takeaway on Friday. A walk on Sunday. Something small.
7. Remind them: we'll figure it out. — Not "it's fine." Just "we're in this together."
8. **Save:** "Save this for when they need it." · "Then quietly start with number two."
