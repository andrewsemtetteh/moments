# Hook Bank (slide 1)

Slide 1 decides whether anyone swipes. Rules: ≤ 10 words, specific, a promise or a tension.
Add new winners to the top of each list.

## Curiosity
- "Questions that made us fall in love all over again"
- "The question that started our biggest (good) conversation"
- "Things couples who last do differently"
- "Nobody talks about this part of long distance"
- "We tried this for 7 days and it changed everything"

## Specific + practical (saves)
- "10 date ideas under $20"
- "Cozy fall date ideas you haven't tried"
- "At-home date nights that don't feel lazy"
- "What to do when you've run out of date ideas"
- "Rainy day dates for couples who hate going out"

## Relatable (shares / tags)
- "Signs you're dating your best friend"
- "Things only long distance couples understand"
- "Tell me you've been together 3+ years without telling me"
- "Relationship green flags nobody mentions"
- "If your partner does these, keep them"

## Send-to-partner (shares)
- "Send this to your person and answer #4"
- "Questions to ask your partner tonight"
- "Make your partner answer these"
- "Your partner owes you answers to these"

## Interactive (comments)
- "Couples This or That — comment your answers"
- "Rate your relationship from these 6 slides"
- "Pick one. Your partner picks one. Compare."

## Psychology (authority)
- "How to love someone whose love language is acts of service"
- "Your attachment style explains your arguments"
- "What to say after an argument (that actually helps)"

## Product (Sundays)
- "We built the app we wished we had in long distance"
- "The app that put my boyfriend on my home screen"
- "Our 100-day streak started with this app"
- "Apps every couple should download in 2026"
