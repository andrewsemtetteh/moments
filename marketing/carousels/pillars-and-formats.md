# Pillars & Formats

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Pillar | Why it works | Primary metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Date ideas** | Highest save rate of any couple content | Saves |
| Tue | **Questions to ask** | People send them to their partner | Shares |
| Wed | **Long distance** | Underserved, emotional, loyal niche; Moments fits perfectly | Saves + follows |
| Thu | **Relationship psychology** (love languages, attachment, conflict) | "This is so us" → tags & shares | Shares |
| Fri | **Play / interactive** (This or That, quizzes, would you rather) | Weekend energy, comment bait | Comments |
| Sat | **Weekend plans** (date ideas by budget/season/at-home) | Timely, saveable | Saves |
| Sun | **Moments spotlight** (soft or direct app post) or **seasonal** | Converts the week's attention into downloads | Profile visits / installs |

Seasonal moments override the rhythm (Halloween, cuffing season, Christmas, Valentine's, anniversaries).

## Formats

Each format = a fixed slide recipe. Design it once in Canva, reuse forever.
`{braces}` are variables — swap them to generate new posts.

### F1 — The List ("{N} {type} date ideas for {context}")
1. Hook: `{N} {type} date ideas for {context}`
2–6. One idea per slide: **name** + 1-line "why it's fun" + tiny emoji
7. CTA: "Save this for your next date night ✨"

Variables: `type` = cheap / cozy / at-home / rainy day / first date / adventurous / free · `context` = fall, winter, this weekend, broke couples, introverts, long distance, after a long week

### F2 — Questions to ask ("{N} questions to ask your partner {when}")
1. Hook
2–7. One question per slide, big type
8. CTA: "Send this to your person and answer #3 👀"

Variables: `when` = before bed, on a road trip, on date night, when you're bored, after a fight, before moving in, on your anniversary, to feel closer

### F3 — Signs / This is us ("Signs you've found {thing}")
1. Hook: "Signs you've found your person" / "Signs your relationship is secure"
2–6. One relatable sign per slide
7. CTA: "Tag them if this is you two"

### F4 — Before / After (habit change)
1. Hook: "Things we stopped doing that saved our relationship"
2–6. "❌ old habit → ✅ new habit"
7. CTA

### F5 — This or That (interactive)
1. Hook: "Couples This or That — comment your answers"
2–7. Two options per slide, split layout
8. "Comment your answers + make your partner do it"
(In-app tie-in: Moments has *This or That* — play it with your partner.)

### F6 — Love language / psychology explainer
1. Hook: "How to love someone whose love language is {X}"
2–6. One concrete action per slide
7. CTA: "Take the love language quiz together in Moments"

### F7 — Long distance survival guide
1. Hook: "{N} things that make long distance easier" / "LDR things only we understand"
2–6. One tip/relatable truth per slide
7. CTA: "Countdown to your next visit 🫶 (we built Moments for this)"

### F8 — The Challenge ("7-day {X} challenge for couples")
1. Hook
2–8. Day 1 → Day 7, one action each
9. CTA: "Start today. Track your streak in Moments 🔥"
(In-app tie-in: *7 Day Connection* pack + streaks.)

### F9 — Apps / tools list (soft sell)
1. Hook: "Apps every couple should have in {year}" / "Apps that saved our long distance"
2–5. Mix of real tools (shared notes, Spotify blend, flights tracker…) with Moments as one slide
6. CTA: "Which one are you downloading?"

### F10 — App spotlight (direct sell)
1. Hook: "We made an app for couples who {pain}"
2–5. One feature per slide, phone screenshot + 1 line (widget, streaks, games, shared calendar)
6. CTA: "Moments — free on the App Store"

### F11 — Conversation screenshot / POV
1. Hook: "POV: you open your phone and your partner left you this" (widget screenshot)
2–5. Story told through mock widget/chat screenshots
6. CTA

### F12 — Seasonal / calendar
1. Hook: "Couple bucket list for {season/holiday}"
2–8. One item per slide
9. CTA: "Add these to your shared calendar"

## Scaling math

12 formats × ~10 variable values each = **120+ distinct posts** before repeating a format+topic combo —
four months of daily posting. Then winners get remade with new hooks.
